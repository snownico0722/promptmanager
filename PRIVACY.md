# Privacy Policy

**Open Prompt Manager** (the Chrome extension)

Effective date: **14 September 2026**

**Your prompt library stays on your computer.** The extension does not use analytics, advertising, or an account for its core features.

---

## What stays on your device

Core use does **not** require an account, email address, or sign-in. Your library and settings stay in the extension on this browser profile.

- **Library** — titles, content, tags, and folders
- **Settings** — theme, shortcut, launcher mode, and which assistant sites you enabled
- JSON backups you export yourself

Your **prompt library is not synced** to a remote service. Use export / import to copy prompts to another device.

Uninstalling the extension deletes this storage from that browser profile.

---

## Permissions

Site access is **optional**. The extension does not need access to websites to install or to manage your library.

It can:

- Save your library and settings on this device
- Open the full-page prompt manager and work with tabs you explicitly enable
- Insert a prompt or save a selection on sites you have allowed

On a site you enabled, the extension looks for the chat input so it can insert a prompt you picked. It does not upload page content, chat history, or cookies. After you insert a prompt, that assistant’s own privacy policy applies.

---

## What we do not do

- No analytics or advertising in the extension
- No sale of personal information
- No login or email requirement for the local library
- No remote upload of your prompt library or full backup
- No reading of your AI chats
- No public prompt catalog, community import, or prompt publishing feature

---

## Your choices

- Export or delete prompts in the extension; uninstall to remove local data for that profile
- Revoke site access in the extension or in Chrome’s extension details

---

## Children

This product is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

---

## Changes

We will update this page when the product changes in a way that affects privacy. The effective date at the top will change.
